## 2. Category and positioning (APPROVED)
AI guest concierge for short-term-rental hosts. *Show Alfred your place once; he answers your guests on WhatsApp, Telegram or the web, and calls you in only when it matters.* Promise: **time back**, and easy for people who are not technical (PMS complexity is the pain). Tagline anchor (from ROADMAP): "Give yourself the gift of time" — Spanish working line **"Regálate tiempo."** (placeholder; native copywriting still to do).

## 3. Audience
Both solo owners (1-5 homes) and small property managers. Non-technical. **Spanish first** — the beta market is Mexico, WhatsApp-first. Simple intuitive words only ("Train now", "autopilot", "step in"; never ingest/scrape/merge/knowledge base).

## 4. Archetype and personality
**The discreet butler** (Caregiver in voice): there when needed, never intrudes, anticipates. Visual atmosphere is "night shift / a lamp left on". Calm, quiet, assured, warm, plain-spoken.

## 5. Distinctive brand assets
1. **The lit doorway** — arch + gold door floating over its reflection (logo P2). Not a letter.
2. **Lantern Gold used only for "a person is needed"** on a dark purple ground (hand-off, primary button, door light).
3. **A soft purple glow around cards and pop-up windows in the dark theme** (light theme: no glow; windows get an Aubergine Ink hairline and inner cards a faint purple outline, because the glow looked dirty on bone), plus the night-hero → bone-section structure of pages.

## 6. Core metaphor and logo
Threshold: someone is home and the light is on. Logo **P2** locked — spec and SVGs in `brand-assets/logo/`. Arch = text colour (Pearl on dark, Aubergine Ink on light), **never purple**; door + glow = Lantern Gold in both themes; flat version below ~40px and for the favicon. Rejected: any letter/M monogram (the founder wants none), arch-A crossbar, bell, open ring, floor-pool smudge, ochre/brass door.

## 7. Palette (LOCKED) — see `brand-assets/palette.md` and `tokens.json`
Blend: Violet Hour 75% / Dusk Plum 25%. **Dark:** Aubergine `#160C24` canvas, Plum Glass `#25153C` surface, Pearl `#F4EFF8` text, Lilac Mist `#BCA9D5` muted, Amethyst `#AB81F2` brand/glow, Lilac `#BA96F6` purple text, Celadon `#90CEA8` status. **Light:** Bone `#F4ECE0` canvas, Bone Light `#FCF8F1` surface, Aubergine Ink `#1B102C` text, Dusk Violet `#5E4D75` muted, Deep Amethyst `#6235AF` brand/glow/links, Deep Celadon `#2E6A4C` status. **Emergency red (added 2026-10-03):** Óxido, dark `#DB6D63` / light `#A6382E`, only for urgent or failed states, always with an icon and label. **Both:** Lantern Gold `#F4B64C` (the only warm light/hue for hand-off; Óxido is the only red). All text pairs ≥ 4.5:1 (lowest 5.1).

## 8. Voice pillars (APPROVED)
1. Plain words a non-technical host already uses; name the action ("Train now", "Take over").
2. Calm and specific; no hype, no "24/7", "seamless", "AI-powered".
3. Alfred is "he"; he reports, asks, never boasts.
4. Natural Mexican Spanish (tú), written natively — not translated.
5. Always give the host control: they see what Alfred says and can take over any time.

## 9. Category clichés and competitors to avoid
Teal/blue/coral SaaS styling, robot/sparkle/chat-bubble icons, "24/7 instant replies", the old purple-blue-mint aurora identity. Benchmarked live 2026-10-02: Airbnb (coral `#ff385c`), Alfred Hospitality AI (white + coral-red, from $39.99/mo/property), Booking/Hilton (navy), Marriott (red), Four Seasons (deep green, serif display), Aman (bone neutrals), Hostaway (orange/teal), Guesty (green band), Hospitable (violet band). Every measured site had a light canvas — a dark-first brand is a deliberate code-break.

## 10. Hero features (user-facing)
1. Show Alfred your place (paste the listing link, add notes/documents). 2. **Train now.** 3. Leave it on **autopilot** (WhatsApp, Telegram, web chat). 4. **Step in** only when it matters. Landing goal: **waitlist**. Landing is **Spanish first**, standalone folder inside this project.

## 11. Typography (LOCKED 2026-10-04, brand amendment)
**Marcellus** (a single regular weight, 400) for the wordmark, tagline and display headlines; **Manrope** (500-700) for body copy and every UI element (buttons, chips, forms, chat messages, captions). Replaces Cormorant Garamond 700 (locked 2026-10-02; the founder found it too generic: it is the most common "luxury serif", and Fraunces, the runner-up, is a face the taste skill bans as an AI favourite). Marcellus has no bold or italic: never synthesize them (`font-synthesis: none`); hierarchy comes from size, case and spacing, and Manrope carries bold UI text. Set as real type, never baked into generated images; the logo's reflection fades out above the letters, no overlap. Study: `brand-assets/concept/type-study-v2.png`.

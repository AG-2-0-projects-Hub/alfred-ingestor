Host Setup Guide

Getting Alfred live on your first property

Six steps from creating your account to watching Alfred answer a real guest question. Each one takes a couple of minutes — the only slow part is the training step, which runs itself.

One thing you'll see before Step 1: the first time you log in with no properties yet, Alfred shows this exact walkthrough on screen — so if you lose this page, the app will remind you where you left off.

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email and a password. This becomes your host login — the one place you'll manage every property and every guest conversation.

2

Add your property

From your dashboard, add a property and paste in its Airbnb listing URL. Alfred reads the public listing automatically — amenities, house rules, description, photos — so you don't have to retype anything that's already public.

3

Upload what Alfred should know

The listing alone won't cover everything a guest asks at 11pm. Add anything that fills the gaps — the more specific, the better Alfred's answers.

What trains Alfred best
  • Your house manual or welcome-book
  • A photo of the WiFi router — name + password
  • How your appliances work — thermostat, washer, coffee machine
  • Check-in / check-out steps and house rules
  • Parking or building access notes
  • A voice note for anything hands-on — towels, linens, the washer, how guests get in

Whatever's easiest — photo, PDF, document, or just tell me in a voice note.

4

Train Alfred

Tap INGEST NOW to have Alfred read everything you uploaded, then MERGE NOW to combine it with the listing into one knowledge base.

If something disagrees If your files and the listing conflict on a detail — say, a different WiFi password — Alfred will flag it and ask you which one is correct before finishing training. Pick the right answer for each, and Alfred takes it from there.
5

Generate a guest link

From the property card, create a link for a guest — real or a test booking. Depending on what's set up, you'll get up to three — send whichever your guest prefers:

  • Guest link (web)
  • Guest link (WhatsApp)
  • Guest link (Telegram)

All three reach the same Alfred, grounded in that specific stay.

Coming soon Native Airbnb inbox integration is in development. Until it rolls out, sharing one of the links above is how a guest reaches Alfred.
6

Test it yourself

Open one of the guest links and chat with Alfred exactly as your guest will. Ask something only a guest would — where's the nearest grocery store, how does the shower work, what's the WiFi password — and see what comes back.

Not happy with an answer? Go back to Step 3, add the missing detail, and retrain. Alfred keeps learning from what actually gets asked.

That's it. Once trained, Alfred handles guest messages on autopilot — you'll only be pulled in when something genuinely needs a human.
Property Enhancement

Keeping Alfred sharp after launch

Training isn't a one-time step. Every property has three tabs for keeping Alfred current, any time.

Overview

Status, your Airbnb link, and a language setting — send the welcome message in English too, alongside the local language.

Files

Everything Alfred has ingested so far. "Edit Property / Add Files" lets you upload more and retrain any time.

Knowledge

The most hands-on tab — three ways to shape what Alfred knows:

  • Ask the Knowledge Base — chat directly with Alfred to see exactly what he'd tell a guest, without waiting for a real one to ask.
  • Add New Knowledge — type, or record a voice note, to add or correct a detail. Updates immediately, no re-ingest needed.
  • Automated Learning — review Q&A pairs Alfred drafts from resolved guest escalations. Accept adds them to the permanent Vault, Discard drops them.

See something confusing, or found a bug? Use the feedback icon in the dashboard — top right, next to your profile — any time.

Alfred, states

One pose, three states. Lit room is the product level and Intenso is the landing level. The red light is new: Óxido, for emergencies only. The matrix below is the glance test, the three states at 1x and 2x, night and day, at both levels.

Glance test

Where this lands

  • Product: Lit room. Landing: Intenso. At small sizes Intenso identifies an escalation best, so it stays a candidate for the product's small escalation icon, to be decided later.
  • Red is Óxido, for urgent or failed only. It never decorates, and it never appears without an icon and a label ("Urgente"). It uses the same pose as Needs you, so the lantern colour is the only change.
  • Gold still means a person is needed. Autopilot has no light at all.
  • The emergency pulse is faster than the gold one but stays under two flashes per second, and both stop under reduced motion.

Alfred, sprite study

Three ways to give Alfred a pixel body. Each one is drawn on a small grid using only the locked palette. Press Manifest to watch the pixel-in, drag the slider to scrub it the way scrolling will, and switch the state to see where gold appears.

100%

Recommendation

Use A as Alfred, and use C for the moment he manifests.

A is the only one that works as a recurring mascot. It has a body, a face and hands, so it can blink, raise the lantern when you are needed and sit at avatar size beside his chat bubbles. Its face is the weakest part of the drawing and would get a polish pass before it ships.

C belongs in the hero. The arch is the logo, the door is the gold light, and the butler appears from behind as the doorway lights up. That follows the "people only from behind" rule exactly.

B is the cleanest at small sizes and the strongest single idea: the flame turns gold when you are needed. I would keep it as the lantern A carries rather than a fourth character.

Your call: A with C as the hero moment (my pick), B alone, C alone, or another mix. Nothing is final until you pick; all of this was drawn in code and cost nothing.

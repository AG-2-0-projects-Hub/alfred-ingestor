One continuous slow dolly shot, no cuts. On a stone terrace at twilight the camera drifts slowly toward the glowing lantern beside the balustrade, while the two hands rest still around the ceramic cup as the camera moves. The lantern's warm light grows until the whole frame is soft glowing cream, then recedes to reveal a rough plaster wall at night with one iron lantern glowing and the left side of the frame quiet and empty. The camera slows to a gentle stop. Cinematic, shallow depth of field, matte materials, aubergine and violet shadows, fine film grain, steady slow motion.

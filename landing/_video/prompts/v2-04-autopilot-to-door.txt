One continuous slow dolly shot, no cuts. The camera follows the traveller forward into the stone archway, the aged stone walls sliding past on both sides. The warm glow ahead grows until the whole frame is soft glowing cream, then recedes, and the camera arrives in front of two closed dark old wooden doors with a hairline of golden light along the seam, a hand resting on the ring of a brass key already in the iron lock. The camera slows to a gentle stop. Cinematic, warm practical light, aubergine shadows, matte aged wood, fine film grain, steady slow motion.

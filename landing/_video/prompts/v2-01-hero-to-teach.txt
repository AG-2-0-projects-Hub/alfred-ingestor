One continuous slow dolly shot, no cuts. The camera glides forward through the glowing arched doorway of a stone courtyard at dusk. The golden light grows until the whole frame is soft glowing cream, then recedes, and the camera arrives on a stone terrace at twilight where two hands already rest around a ceramic cup on a weathered stone balustrade, a phone beside them and a glowing lantern at the edge of the frame, soft violet hills in the haze behind. The camera slows to a gentle stop. Cinematic, shallow depth of field, warm practical light, aubergine and violet shadows, matte materials, fine film grain, steady slow motion.

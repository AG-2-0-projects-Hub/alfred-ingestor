# Claude Design brief: Mayordommo, "Lit Doorway"

**Company:** Mayordommo (public brand). **Alfred** is the in-product butler ("he").

**Blurb (in the brand's voice):** Show Alfred your place once. He answers your guests on WhatsApp, Telegram or the web, and calls you in only when it matters. Go live your life; the light stays on.

**Design-system name:** Lit Doorway. Metaphor: someone is home and the lamp is on. Logo: an arch with a gold door floating over its own reflection (never a letter or monogram).

**Palette**
- Dark (default): Aubergine `#160C24` canvas · Plum Glass `#25153C` surface · Pearl `#F4EFF8` text and logo arch · Lilac Mist `#BCA9D5` muted · Lantern Gold `#F4B64C` hand-off, primary button, door light · Amethyst `#AB81F2` brand, glow · Lilac `#BA96F6` purple text · Celadon `#90CEA8` status · Óxido `#DB6D63` urgent or failed only.
- Light (bone sections): Bone `#F4ECE0` canvas · Bone Light `#FCF8F1` surface · Aubergine Ink `#1B102C` text and logo arch · Dusk Violet `#5E4D75` muted · Deep Amethyst `#6235AF` brand and links · Deep Celadon `#2E6A4C` status · Óxido `#A6382E` urgent or failed only · Lantern Gold `#F4B64C` as a chip or button fill only.

**Fonts:** Marcellus (single weight, 400, no bold or italic) for the wordmark and display headlines · Manrope for UI and body.

**Tone:** calm, quiet, warm, plain-spoken, discreet. Natural Mexican Spanish (tú). Plain words ("Train now", "autopilot", "step in"); never ingest, scrape, merge, "24/7" or "AI-powered".

**Do**
- Use gold only for "a person is needed" and the doorway light; use Óxido only for urgent or failed states, always with an icon and a label.
- Dark theme: soft purple glow around cards and pop-ups. Light theme: no glow, an ink hairline on windows and a faint purple outline on inner cards.
- Open pages with a dark night hero (cinematic Mexican architecture at dusk, one lit doorway), then bone sections.

**Don't**
- No letter or M logo, no purple arch, no ochre or brass in the light theme, no teal/coral/blue SaaS styling, no robot or sparkle icons, no text baked into photographs.

Full tokens and component rules: `DESIGN.md`. Logo SVGs: `logo/`. Photography: `web/` and `manifest.md`.
